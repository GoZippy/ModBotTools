# AI policy — ModBotTools

This project is built with AI tools, and we welcome contributions that use them.
These rules keep the quality bar and the accountability where they belong.

1. **A human owns every contribution.** Whoever opens a PR or files an issue
   must understand every line of it and be able to answer review questions
   without going back to the AI. "The model wrote it" is not an answer.
2. **Disclose it.** Tick the right box in the PR template's *AI assistance*
   section and name the tools. Small autocompletions don't count; generated
   functions, tests, docs or designs do.
3. **You are the author.** Don't add AI co-author or "generated by" trailers.
   The human who commits signs off (`git commit -s`) and certifies the
   [DCO](https://developercertificate.org/) — an AI cannot.
4. **No unattended agents from outside the team.** PRs, issues and comments
   opened by an autonomous agent without a human reviewing them first are
   closed without review. The maintainers run their own agents under this
   same policy.
5. **Verify before you report.** AI-found bugs and especially security reports
   must be reproduced by you first. Unverified generated reports are closed,
   and repeat senders are blocked.
6. **Mind the licence.** Don't submit output that reproduces third-party code
   you could not submit by hand.
7. **Good first issues are for people learning the codebase.** Please don't
   solve them with an agent.

Maintainers may ask how a change was produced and may decline contributions
that don't meet this bar. This policy draws on the LLVM, Linux kernel and
Ghostty policies.
